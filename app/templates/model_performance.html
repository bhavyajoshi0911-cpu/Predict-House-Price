Data Leakage Prevention

All preprocessing transformations (StandardScaler for numerical metrics and OneHotEncoder for zipcodes) are bound into a strictly encapsulated scikit-learn Pipeline. Preprocessors are fitted exclusively on the 80% training set and evaluated on the untouched 20% test partition.

R² and Error Metrics

R² (Coefficient of Determination) measures the proportion of market price variance explained by the model features. MAE represents the average dollar deviation. RMSE applies quadratic penalty to outliers, reflecting risk of large discrepancies.

Defensible Prediction Interval

Uncertainty bounds are calculated using an empirical 90% prediction interval based on the held-out test RMSE ($\pm 1.645 \times \text{RMSE}$). HomeValue AI avoids fabricated confidence percentages and adheres to transparent statistical error limits.
